## About me

I'm a full-stack developer and the founder of **Stachub Technologies**, a Ghanaian technology company focused on useful, accessible digital solutions. I build web applications, business software and product experiences, with a particular interest in SaaS, marketplaces and systems that work for everyday users.

I enjoy taking products from concept to implementation: designing interfaces, developing APIs, modelling data, integrating services and improving reliability as systems grow.

- **Currently building:** StacMart, RetailTrack and other products at Stachub Technologies.
- **Currently exploring:** scalable backend architecture, mobile development, offline-first applications and AI integration.
- **Open to:** software engineering collaborations, freelance projects and remote opportunities.
